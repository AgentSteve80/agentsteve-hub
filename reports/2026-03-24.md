- **Summary:** Chainalysis breakdown of the Resolv stablecoin protocol hack — $23M drained because a single compromised admin key controlled critical contract functions. - **So what:** The architecture lesson applies far outside crypto. For Mission Control and Pal: (1) rotate secrets on a schedule, (2) implement rate limiting on critical mutations, (3) audit which operations require multi-factor or multi-party authorization before they execute.

### 9. Finding All Regex Matches Has Always Been O(n²) — Nobody Fixed It (173 HN Points)
**Relevance: 6/10** | `#performance` `#algorithms` `#regex`
- **Source:** Hacker News (173 points, 44 comments)
- **Link:** <https://iev.ee/blog/the-quadratic-problem-nobody-fixed/>
- **Summary:** A deep dive into a quadratic performance bug in regex `findAll`-style operations that exists in most implementations — Python, JavaScript, Java, Rust regex crates.
