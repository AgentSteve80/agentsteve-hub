## 🤖 AI & Inference

### 3. Hypura — Storage-Tier-Aware LLM Inference Scheduler for Apple Silicon (136 HN Points)
**Relevance: 9/10** | `#ai` `#local-inference` `#apple-silicon` `#mac` `#openclaw`
- **Source:** Hacker News #2 (136 points, 60 comments)
- **Link:** <https://github.com/t8/hypura>
- **Summary:** **Hypura** is a new open-source LLM inference scheduler specifically designed for Apple Silicon that is *storage-tier-aware* — it knows the difference between DRAM, unified memory, and SSD tiers, and intelligently schedules model weight loading to maximize throughput. Built on the same flash-streaming principles as Flash-MoE but as a general scheduler layer rather than a model-specific hack.
- **So what:** **Direct value for our Mac Mini M4.** Currently we use Ollama for local inference, which has basic memory management. Hypura would sit between Ollama/llama.cpp and the hardware to optimize when weights stream from SSD vs. stay resident in DRAM. For large models (70B+), this could meaningfully improve throughput. Star and experiment: `git clone github.com/t8/hypura`. Combine with Flash-MoE for the full local inference picture.
