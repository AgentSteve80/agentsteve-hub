### 1. 🚨 DoJ Disrupts 4 IoT Botnets — Record 31.4 Tbps DDoS, 3 Million Devices
**Source:** The Hacker News · DoJ · March 20, 2026
**Relevance: 9/10** | `#security` `#iot` `#ddos` `#botnet` `#critical`

The DoJ, with Canada, Germany, and 15 private sector firms (Cloudflare, AWS, Google, Akamai, Okta, Oracle, PayPal, etc.) disrupted C2 infrastructure for four IoT botnets: AISURU, Kimwolf, JackSkid, and Mossad. This is a liability argument for the company, not just security hygiene.
