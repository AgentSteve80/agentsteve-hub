### 1. 🚨 FBI + CISA: Russian Intelligence Targeting Signal & WhatsApp — Thousands Compromised
**Source:** The Hacker News · FBI Director Kash Patel · March 21, 2026
**Relevance: 9/10** | `#security` `#signal` `#whatsapp` `#phishing` `#critical`

**Official joint FBI/CISA advisory.** Russian intelligence-affiliated actors are running phishing campaigns against Signal and WhatsApp to compromise accounts of high-value targets: current and former US government officials, military personnel, political figures, and journalists. Attack vector: fake group invites and device-linking QR codes. Thousands of accounts already compromised globally. After access, actors read messages, impersonate the victim, and launch further phishing from a trusted identity.

**So what:** Signal is part of our infrastructure (OpenClaw is connected to Signal). Immediate action: **Settings → Linked Devices → remove any unrecognized devices** on both Signal and WhatsApp. Never scan a QR code to "re-verify" or "join a group" from an unexpected link. For field techs: this is the exact attack that could compromise a tech's Signal if someone sends them a fake "job invite" QR code. Add this to the field tech security briefing before rollout.

🔗 https://thehackernews.com/2026/03/fbi-warns-russian-hackers-target-signal.html
